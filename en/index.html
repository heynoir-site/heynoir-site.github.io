What Noir is

A private connection, by subscription

A service that provides access to an encrypted private network channel, for people who work on open networks, travel, or simply want their traffic protected from interception. You subscribe on the site and pay by bank card. Access and setup instructions are issued automatically once payment goes through.

Traffic encryption

Modern encryption between your device and the Noir server. Third-party networks see that a secure connection exists, not what travels through it.

Servers in three countries

Nodes in Germany, the Netherlands and the United States. The client picks the fastest node by latency on its own, so there is nothing to switch by hand.

Your data is not the product

We do not sell connection records to ad networks. Technical logs are kept for no longer than 30 days. The details are in our data processing policy.

Privacy

Clear rules, not promises

Trust in a service is built on rules written down honestly. Three of them are formalised in our data processing policy and terms of service.

What we collect

An account identifier for authentication and billing, an email address where notifications are needed, and anonymised technical metrics about the connection.

What we do not collect

The contents of your traffic: it is encrypted. We keep no browsing logs and we do not tie activity to a person without cause.

Where we process it

Billing and authentication run on our own servers. Metrics are kept for no longer than 30 days. Transfers to third parties are limited to the payment provider and the hosting provider.
